Welcome to Vulpes Tourney!

What is Vulpes Toruney?

Vulpes Tourney is an event that I host for fun similar to Monkey League. Vulpes, or fox in Latin, is the animal name and I chose tourney rather than league because this is more of a tourney than a league.


This tourney is for speedsolving/speedcubing, so it includes all peoples (within reason) and many forms of events.


To get started, select "Live" to see the live results of an ongoing tourney, or scroll down to "Past Tourneys" to view the results of prior events.
